Cutpals

Cut-paper mascots, made by your AI agent.

Download any of these for free, or install the skill and describe your product: your agent cuts six candidates out of colored paper.

npx skills@latest add Longado/cutpals-skill

GitHub · 中文说明

Describe your product

Your agent reads it and proposes three characters, each tied to a different trait.

Get six candidates

Each character in two palettes. A centered head, 3–4 paper layers, two dot eyes, four colors.

Keep the one you like

Nothing is filtered or retouched. Ask for a redraw only if you want one.

Every image here was made with Cutpals or an earlier draft of its rules, using Codex's built-in image tool. Not retouched. Free to download and use.
